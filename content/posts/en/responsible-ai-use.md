AI delivers huge productivity gains, but misuse can cause serious harm to individuals and businesses. Here are the six most common risks and how to avoid each one.

## 1. Leaking sensitive data

Pasting contracts, customer lists or internal source code into a public AI tool can breach company security policy.

**Prevention:**

- Read the tool's data policy: is your data used for training?
- Prefer business plans with clear privacy commitments.
- Anonymise information (replace names and phone numbers with placeholders) before pasting.

## 2. Misinformation (hallucination)

AI can produce figures, quotes or legal references that **sound convincing but don't exist**.

**Prevention:** verify anything important against the original source, especially in legal, medical and financial contexts.

## 3. Bias

Models learn from human data and can inherit prejudices about gender, region or age. Screening CVs with AI without checks can unfairly exclude candidates.

**Prevention:** keep a human reviewing decisions that affect people; test results across different groups.

## 4. Copyright and intellectual property

AI output can resemble copyrighted work, and rules on ownership of AI-generated content are still evolving in many countries.

**Prevention:** don't ask AI to replicate a specific person's work or style for commercial use; disclose when content was produced with AI assistance.

## 5. Deepfakes and scams

Fake voices and videos are increasingly hard to spot. Scammers may imitate a boss or relative to request urgent transfers.

**Prevention:**

- Verify through a **second channel** (call back a known number) for any urgent money request.
- Agree on a family "safe word" or an internal verification process.

## 6. Over-reliance

Outsourcing all your thinking to AI erodes your own skills and makes it harder to notice when the AI is wrong.

**Prevention:** use AI as a **sparring partner**, not a decision-maker. Write the first draft yourself for work that matters.

## Quick checklist before using AI

- [ ] Is the data I'm about to paste sensitive?
- [ ] Can I verify the result?
- [ ] Does this decision affect other people?
- [ ] Do I understand and take responsibility for the final output?

> AI is a tool. **Final responsibility always rests with people.**
